# Week-11-Dungeon-Crawler
Plan It:

What is the goal of this website?

To be a 2D pixel RPG, where the player fights enemies (of different types) to earn points to unlock upgrades. They will progress through different floors/levels, where each floor/level is of an increasing difficulty.

Who are you building for, and what do they care about?

People who want to play simple games fast. They want games that conclude in a couple of minutes.

Describe how a player should use the game from the moment they visit the site. What should they see first, what should they click, and what should happen next?

The player will fight endless waves of enemies (for example slimes and goblins). The player must beat a certain number of enemies per floor. When they complete a floor, they move up one floor. There will be abilities and buttons at either the top or bottom of the screen.

How should the dungeon crawler work? (movement, combat, damage, blocking, and goals)

There will be one drop per enemy (mostly upgrades). Currency is spent to recruit allies. The game can be played either online or offline. The floors get progressively harder. The items give the player upgrades (such as increased damage, increased speed, or weapons). The player will fight an enemy automatically upon getting sufficiently close to it.

What words best describe the look and feel that you want?

Make it look like a dungeon by using only brown and gray as colors.

Test It:

Feature a different pose for each unique direction the player is moving in. Only have the character deal damage in front of them.

Expand It:

Make the view smaller. Include a joystick. Make the options pop up.
